## Challenge

A writing team of 8 supporting a product org that shipped features faster than traditional documentation cycles could keep pace with. Review queues backed up, coverage was inconsistent, and writers spent more time on formatting and boilerplate than on the actual content decisions that required human judgment.

## Approach

Designed and built 10 production AI tools scoped to the real friction points in the team's workflow: draft generation from engineering specs, RAG-grounded fact-checking against the existing doc corpus, MCP-connected context retrieval from Jira and Confluence, an agentic review pipeline that flagged style and accuracy issues before human review, and a Slack-native interface so writers could run tools without leaving their existing workflow.

Every tool was scoped around a specific handoff or decision point. Not a general-purpose assistant, but a set of purpose-built accelerators for the moments where writers were losing the most time.

## Results

Documentation cycle times dropped by 80%. The 8-person team maintained coverage across a product surface that would previously have required a significantly larger team. The toolsuite earned a 4.5/5 rating at the Salesforce AI Expo and generated organic demand from Lead and Staff-level writers across the organisation. Tools remained in active production use through the end of the engagement.
