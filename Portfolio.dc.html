About

I make complicated platforms make sense.

The work usually starts the same way: a system that is genuinely hard to explain, a team that is short on time, and developers who need a clear answer fast. I have spent eighteen years getting good at exactly that.

At Salesforce I was selected as the writing team's AI champion. I designed and shipped a suite of ten AI assistants for eight writers, using RAG, MCP, and agentic search to cut first-draft time by 80% while keeping subject-matter approval at every stage. The work was presented at the VP CX quarterly review as part of a company-wide AI initiative.

At Intel I owned developer documentation for the OpenVINO and Tiber Edge AI platforms, writing API references, programming guides, and runnable code samples in Python and C++. The thread across every role is the same: learn the technical detail properly, earn the trust of the engineers, and make the product measurably easier to adopt.
