The challenge

Review cycles were the bottleneck, not the writing.

Eight technical writers were spending much of the week not writing, but waiting: routing drafts for subject-matter review, chasing approvals, reconciling conflicting edits, and re-validating structure against enterprise publishing rules. Every release multiplied the backlog.

The team did not need more headcount. It needed the repetitive, mechanical phases of the documentation lifecycle to take care of themselves, without breaking the strict DITA and DTD constraints the platform required.

80% faster first drafts, and a team that changed how it works.
